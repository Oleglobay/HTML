Team Fortress 2 is a 2007 multiplayer first-person shooter game developed and published by Valve Corporation.

It is the sequel to the 1996 Team Fortress mod for Quake and its 1999 remake, Team Fortress Classic. The game was released in October 2007 as part of The Orange Box for Windows and the Xbox 360, and ported to the PlayStation 3 in December 2007.
